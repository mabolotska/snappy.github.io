What stays on your device

The recipe catalogue, its pictures, your ingredient list, appearance and language choices, free-use counts, and saved recipe cache stay in the app's local storage. The catalogue works offline. You can use Snappy without an account.

Deleting the app removes its local data. iPhone and iCloud backups may keep a copy under your Apple settings.

Photos and recipe ideas

When you scan food, the app sends the photos and a short instruction to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. Gemini returns grocery product names and categories. The app makes the photos smaller before sending them. We do not use your photos for ads, and we do not keep your scan photos after the result is ready.

When you ask for recipes, the app sends the ingredient names you confirmed to Gemini. Gemini returns air-fryer recipes. We do not add your email or account details to these AI requests.

The app asks for camera access before you scan. You can turn it off at any time in iPhone Settings.

Generated recipe images

Some recipe cards ask for a photo that is not in the bundled catalogue. For these, the app sends the recipe ID, title, description, and ingredient names to our recipe image service. If you are signed in, the request also carries your Firebase ID token so the service can apply your account limits. We do not send your camera photos to this service.

Generated images are cached on your device. If you save a generated recipe while signed in, the image can also be stored with that recipe in your account.

Accounts and saved recipes

Snappy starts as a guest session. If you sign in with email, Google, or Apple, Firebase Authentication stores your account identifier, email address, and display name. Password reset uses your email address.

Saved recipes are stored under your account in Firebase Firestore. A saved recipe includes the recipe title, ingredients, steps, image references, and the time you saved it. This lets your favorites appear on your devices.

Open Settings and choose Delete account to remove your Firebase account and its saved recipes. Signing out alone does not delete them.

Usage analytics

Mixpanel and AppsFlyer are configured only in production builds, and only when their keys are present. They receive events such as app open, scan started, scan result, recipe request, recipe open, and recipe saved. Event properties are counts, fixed source or reason labels, and recipe identifiers. We do not send ingredient names, recipe titles, photos, emails, account IDs, or error text in these events.

AppsFlyer helps measure installs and campaigns. On iOS it asks for App Tracking Transparency permission if the question has not been asked already. You can refuse it or change your answer in iPhone Settings. You can still use Snappy either way. Snappy does not show ads. Debug builds send no analytics events.

Crash and diagnostic data

Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry collect crash reports and diagnostic events. These can include app version, iOS version, device model, and stack traces. Sentry is configured not to send default personally identifiable information. We use these reports to find and fix bugs, not for advertising.

Purchases

Settings shows Snappy Pro plan choices. Purchase and restore actions are not connected in this version, so the app does not complete purchases or receive payment details. We plan to use RevenueCat to manage subscription status when purchases launch. RevenueCat is not active in this version. Apple will handle payment under its own privacy policy.

Service providers

We share data only with services that help us run Snappy:

  • Apple — app delivery, Sign in with Apple, and App Store purchases if enabled.
  • RevenueCat — planned subscription status management; not active in this version.
  • Google Firebase — sign-in, saved recipes, app check, crash reports, and Gemini requests.
  • Google Gemini — photo analysis and recipe generation through Firebase AI Logic.
  • Our recipe image service — generated recipe photos.
  • Sentry — crash reports and diagnostics.
  • Mixpanel — app usage analytics.
  • AppsFlyer — install and campaign measurement.

Connections use HTTPS. These services process data for us under their own terms.

Retention and your choices

We do not store your scan photos. Generated images are cached on your device and removed with the app or by the cache limits. Account data stays until you delete your account. Analytics and crash reports are kept by their providers under their retention settings; the app does not set a fixed period.

You can remove ingredients and saved recipes in the app, delete your account in Settings, or delete the app to remove its local data. To ask for access, correction, or deletion of data held by us, email m.bolotska@gmail.com. We may need your account email or another detail to find your records. Please do not send card details or passwords.

Support and children

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use them only to answer you. Snappy is not designed for children under 13.

Changes and contact

We update this page when our practices change. Questions: m.bolotska@gmail.com.

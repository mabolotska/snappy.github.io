Frequent questions

What can Snappy do?

Take a photo of your food and Snappy lists the ingredients it finds. You can check or change that list, then ask for air-fryer recipes. You can also browse the bundled catalogue and filter it by ingredients, time, or tags. The catalogue works offline.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Snappy as a guest. If you sign in with email, Google, or Apple, your saved recipes are stored with your account so they appear on your devices.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in Snappy and choose “Delete account”, then confirm. This removes your Firebase account and the saved recipes stored with it. Signing out alone does not delete them. If you cannot sign in, email us.

Where is my data stored?

Preferences, ingredient lists, and cached recipes stay on your device. Scan photos are sent to Gemini through Firebase AI Logic and are not kept after the result is ready. If you sign in, Firebase Authentication stores your account details and Firestore stores your saved recipes. Crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry. Production builds can send usage events to Mixpanel and install measurement to AppsFlyer. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete local data?

Remove ingredients and saved recipes in the app, or delete the app to erase its local data. Deleting the app does not delete your account or its cloud data. For a data request, email m.bolotska@gmail.com; see the privacy policy for details.

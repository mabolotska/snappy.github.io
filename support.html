Frequent questions

What is free and what is in Nomdo Pro?

All core breakfasts, filters, your ingredients at home, favorites and servings are free. Nomdo Pro adds new breakfasts and themed collections that arrive regularly without an app update, and collections by protein, calories, sugar, fiber and carbs.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You can cancel at any time; the period you already paid for runs to its end. A free trial cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends is not charged.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings in Nomdo and tap “Restore purchases”.

Where is my data stored?

Your ingredients and personal recipe content stay on your device; no account is required. Your full favorites list stays on your device. After you install Nomdo 1.5 or later, individual recipe openings and favorite changes include the public recipe ID; aggregate recent favorites also form the shared Popular ranking, without publishing user identifiers or personal lists. Apple sends purchase events to RevenueCat, which also forwards them to our server and the existing ActivationPal integration. Versions 1.2 and later send usage events to our own server, where they are retained for 90 days; setup answers and reminder settings stay on your device. Version 1.1 sends usage events to ActivationPal; version 1.0 does not send app usage events. The usage-event retention limit does not apply to purchase records. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete all data?

Remove ingredients and favorites in the app, or delete the app to erase its local data. This does not cancel a subscription or delete purchase and analytics records already held by service providers. For a data request, email artsaverin@gmail.com; see the privacy policy for details.
